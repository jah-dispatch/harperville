The Taylor Dedication

Surviving life requires radical autonomy.

...and an immaculate, unrelenting discography appreciation.

Out of the Woods

This has recently become my anthem as I navigate the complex recovery of my neck/spine fusion. It's not been the easiest road, and there are unexplained pains here and there still, but I can at least look to the sky and know that just on Day 12 post-op, I was going to be all right and I was starting to emerge from out of the woods.

ME!

From picturesque color splashes to the unapologetic energy of the music video, this track embodies the essence of self-empowerment and confidence.

The Fate of Ophelia

It took me a little time to adjust to this music video only because I briefly un-became a Swiftie momentarily throughout the fall of last year. I've realized it was just the pain and discomfort my back was going through that dictated my moods. Soon enough, I designed a T shirt based off this song and I can't wait to wear it again. But first, I need to FIT in it again.

Style

This one's all about the beats. And the visual artistry with all the fractals of illusions and clipping mask effects. It takes the cake on my top Taylor videos.

This is Why We Can't Have Nice Things

Whhile technically not a music video, the choreography and set tells a perfectly compelling story about extending one's worth and efforts only to receive pittance in return. This is the ultimate anthem to reclaiming self-autonomy and demand dignity by controlling your own boundaries and rewrite your narrative.
